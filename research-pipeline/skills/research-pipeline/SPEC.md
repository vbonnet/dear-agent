## EARS Requirements

**DECL-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-SKILL-01** When a harness loads the research-pipeline skill, the system shall expose its trigger description and workflow from the co-located `SKILL.md`.

**DECL-RESEARCH-PIPELINE-SKILL-02** When the skill's trigger/behavior cases are evaluated, the system shall check them against the co-located `evals.json`.

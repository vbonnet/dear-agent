# SPEC Audit Inventory and Report Specification

**Status:** Active
**Scope:** Deterministic pinned inventory, finding validation, and offline audit
artifact production.

This specification owns the observable `specaudit` command behavior. It does
not own semantic consolidation decisions or the replaceable implementation
used to obtain those outcomes.

## EARS Requirements

**SPECAUDIT-01** When inventory receives an explicit repository identity and exact Git revision, the artifact shall account for every tracked `SPEC.md` and BDD feature in that revision's Git objects exactly once and shall order all records deterministically without consulting working-tree content.

**SPECAUDIT-02** When inventory interprets a specification or BDD feature, the artifact shall exclude fenced examples from normative counts, derive identified requirements from canonical Markdown-normalized EARS text, accept only a documented full-line traceability bullet with one backtick-delimited repository-relative `.feature` path inside a supported H2 traceability section across all documented heading spellings, shall permit multiple supported sections when at most one is feature-bearing, shall treat a valid or malformed `.feature` claim as making its section feature-bearing, shall use the first feature-bearing section as the sole source of links and diagnose and ignore feature claims in every later feature-bearing section without combining links, and shall diagnose anonymous or nonconforming requirements and missing, malformed, duplicate, ambiguous, or nonreciprocal SPEC/BDD links.

**SPECAUDIT-03** When report validation or rendering receives an inventory, the command shall recompute the complete inventory from the named Git objects at the pinned revision and shall reject any supplied inventory, finding set, or zero-finding result that does not match; semantic reports shall omit `inventory`, `features`, and `seeds` and shall use only the separately supplied pinned inventory.

**SPECAUDIT-04** When report validation checks a positive finding, the command shall accept only canonical repository-relative paths without aliases such as `./` or cleaned traversal, shall require the current-owner set to equal the pinned requirement-evidence owners, shall derive a bounded adapter-scope catalog from pinned active and deprecated harness declarations, normalization aliases, authoritative repo-local configuration-directory mappings, the pinned Claude-native marketplace mirror path, and compatibility-adapter identity, shall reject every selected catalog-metadata Go blob above its 256 KiB per-source ceiling before reading its body and shall parse each admitted projection without object resolution, shall check the inventory context before and after each synchronous bounded projection without claiming strict mid-parse preemption, shall scan the complete pinned registry AST and accept `activeHarnesses` and `deprecatedHarnesses` only when each target occurs exactly once as a supported package `[]string` declaration without any other lexical binding or direct, recursive, range, or increment/decrement assignment target, shall retain exact pinned evidence for every non-identifier catalog name, shall not classify the authoritative neutral marketplace path as adapter-local, shall fail closed with the exact incomplete-catalog limitation and empty adapter/member scopes when normalization-alias, configuration-directory, marketplace, or other required metadata is absent or source metadata uses an unsupported, duplicate, or ambiguous AST shape, shall forbid every positive recommendation while any source-catalog limitation remains, shall reject a proposed owner whose path matches the complete catalog without classifying arbitrary `internal` or `pkg` directories, shall reject a new owner nested beneath a current owner, and shall require separate ownership and harness-neutrality rationales; `merge-now` shall require at least two current owners and one existing proposed owner, while a one-owner `extract-neutral-contract` shall require one new proposed owner and retirement of the current owner.

**SPECAUDIT-05** When report validation checks BDD impact with one or more selected features, the command shall require every selected feature to exist at the pinned revision and to reciprocally reference at least one current owner, shall require every current owner to be reciprocally covered by at least one selected feature, and for every positive finding regardless of classification shall require one bounded applicability basis plus one selected feature that reciprocally references every current owner and an exact pinned scenario-outline reference with at least one observable outcome, an exercised `harness` or `member` placeholder, and unique examples cases that exactly cover at least two applicability members whose disposition is not `not-applicable`, while diagnosing and rejecting that selected outline if it mixes harness/member Examples tables with any additional Examples table that lacks a harness/member column; `active-members` applicability shall exactly cover the full pinned active-member set, `implementation-only` applicability shall name at least two implementations and cite every current owner across that matrix without bypassing a harness contract's active-member scope, and when no feature is selected the command shall require a non-positive finding with BDD consequence `none`.

**SPECAUDIT-06** When rendering a validated audit artifact, the command shall emit self-contained offline HTML that safely represents evidence and retains every decision field, owner topology, proposed-owner neutrality rationale, pending ownership and preservation plan, applicability row, adapter-scope catalog and its pinned citations, BDD path and exact shared-contract scenario, the selected scenario's pinned kind, every outcome line and text, member column, member-placeholder use, and every case line, member, and source, exclusion, methodology fact including the fixed runtime status `UNVERIFIED` and every Git trust-input identity, and limitation, including every applicability and requirement-preservation citation's source path, line, requirement identifier, and excerpt even when that citation is absent from finding-level evidence.

**SPECAUDIT-07** When inventory or rendering succeeds, the command shall emit exactly one complete artifact to standard output, and when validation, collection, or rendering fails, it shall emit no artifact bytes and shall not create, replace, or delete an artifact destination.

**SPECAUDIT-08** When an audit names a comparison revision, the artifact shall label it as comparison-only unless that revision has its own complete pinned inventory.

**SPECAUDIT-09** When the collector resolves pinned Git evidence, the artifact shall record privacy-preserving concrete identities for the canonical caller-approved Git executable, Git-resolved worktree root, repository Git directory, common directory, object directory, and every configured alternate object directory, together with the caller-supplied repository label and exact revision; validation shall recompute those inputs rather than treat them as independently authenticated source truth.

**SPECAUDIT-10** When the collector resolves pinned Git evidence, only the caller-approved Git toolchain and target object-store routing shall influence object selection, and network access, lazy fetching, replacement objects, prompts, the working directory, and unapproved inherited repository or configuration settings shall not supply or redirect evidence.

**SPECAUDIT-11** When inventory emits candidate seeds, the artifact shall derive them deterministically from equal normalized requirement bodies, repeated requirement identifiers, shared BDD references, identical full `SPEC.md` bodies, and harness terminology and shall not contain a semantic relationship, disposition, or canonical-owner verdict.

**SPECAUDIT-12** When validation or rendering reads JSON input, the command shall reject invalid UTF-8 before decoding and shall accept only one stable JSON value whose exact-case schema keys are unique and whose nesting, streaming token count, aggregate element count, aggregate decoded-string bytes, individual strings, and explicit per-field collection counts and string bytes remain within declared ceilings, including the report-level and finding-level `limitations` fields; it shall reject trailing content, input that changes during the read, or any ceiling breach before materializing the typed report.

**SPECAUDIT-13** When inventory JSON or rendered HTML would exceed its declared output ceiling, or any selected Git path, selected textual blob, excerpt, or generated text is invalid UTF-8, the command shall reject the result before writing any artifact bytes; inventory JSON encoding shall enforce that ceiling while escaping untrusted values, shall reject rather than replace invalid bytes, and shall not first materialize an unbounded escaped document.

**SPECAUDIT-14** When collection cannot account for the complete pinned corpus within its declared file-count, aggregate-byte, BDD-feature-byte, Gherkin line, structural-token, retained-tag item/byte, comment count/byte, description line/byte, DocString node/separator/content, scenario, step, outcome, Examples, Examples-case, table-row, table-cell, output-byte, and wall-time ceilings, the command shall fail with no inventory artifact rather than return a partial result; each selected feature shall pass its byte ceiling before object reading and a context-aware wrapper around the official dialect matcher, scanner, and AST builder shall enforce line, language-aware structural, and AST-retained nonstructural ceilings before each token is delegated into the AST builder, shall distinguish DocString content from descriptions and table rows, shall stop at the first parser error, and shall stop AST delegation after the first resource, cancellation, or builder failure while preserving ordinary syntax errors as diagnostics; the parsed AST shall defensively recount scenarios, steps, Examples, cases, rows, cells, outcomes, scenario-name bytes, retained tags, comments, descriptions, and DocString nodes, delimiters, and content, and every ceiling breach shall be rejected rather than truncated.

**SPECAUDIT-15** When report validation or rendering succeeds, the artifact shall distinguish pinned-object evidence and deterministic structural validation from reviewer-authored semantic recommendations, shall require both inventory and semantic methodology to carry the matching fixed runtime status `UNVERIFIED`, shall require every positive finding to retain exactly one existing neutral owner or plan one new neutral owner while mapping every pinned requirement in each retiring specification by exact path, line, identifier, and excerpt together with every pinned reciprocal BDD feature owned by that retiring specification, whether or not the finding selected that feature and always including the positive finding's shared-contract feature, plus exact applicability, and shall accept only `pending-maintainer-approval` so the report cannot authorize consolidation, deletion, or a runtime-parity claim.

**SPECAUDIT-16** When inventory, validation, or rendering runs, the target repository, working tree, Git index and references, specifications, BDD features, issue state, and delivery state shall remain unchanged, and the command shall not apply a consolidation recommendation.

## About ADRs

Architecture Decision Records capture important architectural decisions made during the project, along with their context and consequences. Each ADR follows this structure:

- **Date**: When the decision was made
- **Status**: Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, or Superseded
- **Context**: What situation led to this decision
- **Decision**: What we decided to do
- **Rationale**: Why we made this decision
- **Consequences**: What results from this decision (positive, negative, neutral)
